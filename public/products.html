SDE SMS

Business texting for your whole team.

Text customers from your company’s own numbers, from any browser or phone, with your team working the same conversations.

  • Two-way texting on your business numbers
  • Name each number (Main, Accounting, Service) and choose which team members can use it
  • Text blasts: one message to many customers, now or scheduled, with delivered and failed counts
  • Contacts with spreadsheet import, plus private team chat
  • Installs on your phone with notifications for new messages
  • STOP replies and blocked numbers are handled automatically

Text blasts go only to people who have agreed to receive texts from your business.

SDE Web Fax

Send faxes from your browser.

No fax machine, no phone line, no paper jams. Upload the document, pick the recipient, send.

  • A cover page with your logo and a memo, added automatically
  • Saved fax contacts, so recipients are a click away
  • Fax history for you or your whole company, with reports you can export to Excel
  • Multiple users under one company account
